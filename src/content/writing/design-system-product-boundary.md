A component library becomes a design system when teams can rely on its
boundaries—not merely its inventory.

## Boundaries create leverage

A system should make ownership, supported use cases, and extension points
obvious. Those constraints reduce duplicated decisions across product teams.

## Contribution is part of the product

Documentation, review standards, release notes, and migration paths determine
whether contributors can improve the system without fragmenting it.

## Measure adoption through outcomes

Component usage is useful, but delivery time, accessibility defects, and product
consistency reveal whether the system is producing real leverage.
