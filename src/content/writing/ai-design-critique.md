AI can make critique more frequent, but frequency alone does not make critique
more useful.

## Use agents to widen the evidence

Agents can identify repeated patterns, compare implementation with system
guidance, and surface accessibility or consistency risks before a review.

## Keep judgment accountable

Product context, organizational constraints, and the consequences of a decision
still require people who can explain and own the tradeoff.

## Improve the conversation

The best use of AI is not generating a verdict. It is preparing sharper
questions so a team can spend its time on the decisions that require judgment.
